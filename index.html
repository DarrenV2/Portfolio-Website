AI Resume Analyzer

A web app that checks a resume against a job description, one requirement at a time. Each result is backed by exact quotes from the resume and an honest suggestion, not a made-up match score. Gemini's replies are validated with Pydantic before they're shown, and the backend is tested offline with pytest.
